HOW WE ACTUALLY WORK
WE’LL TELL YOU
WHAT WE KNOW.
And we’ll tell you when we don’t know. That’s the whole methodology, and it’s the reason you can trust the parts we do show you.
WHAT YOU CAN CHECK YOURSELF
Every price is a real price at a real book. Go look.
Every rate comes with its sample size attached.
Every timestamp is when we actually saw it.
Every bet gets its watch-out, not just its case.
WHAT WE WON’T FAKE
We won’t promise you a winning season.
We won’t show you a win rate we made up.
We won’t print a probability we can’t defend.
We won’t invent a concern to look thorough.
WHERE OUR RESEARCH ACTUALLY SITS
Everything in the product right now is observation — real data, described plainly. Nothing has been forward tested yet, so nothing gets sold to you as proven. When something earns a higher rung, we’ll move it up and say so.
If ranked opportunities or model numbers ever hold up under real testing, we’ll ship them. We’re just not going to pretend we’re there yet.
And when there’s genuinely nothing to flag on a bet, we say that. “Nothing here worries us. The number is the number.” A made-up downside is just as dishonest as a made-up edge.

FAIR QUESTIONS
01 So do you tell me what to bet?
Not right now. We show you the case, the thing that could burn you, and where the number is better. If ranked plays ever survive real testing, we’ll ship them and tell you exactly what changed.
02 Do you guarantee winners?
No. Nobody can, and anyone who says otherwise is selling you something. What we can do is stop you giving away points and betting into news you hadn’t seen.
03 What happens after my three free checks?
They’re three in total, not three a day. After that it’s Founding Access at $19.99 a month, cancel anytime.
04 What data are you actually using?
Prices from eleven sportsbooks, official lineups, schedules, bullpen usage and park data. Every rate ships with its sample size so you can judge it yourself.
05 Why no win percentages or EV numbers?
Because we’d be making them up. Everything on screen is something you could go and verify in about a minute. That’s the bar.
06 How do I cancel?
Settings, billing, cancel. Two clicks, no phone call, confirmation lands in your inbox straight away.
