10 SCREEN STATES LOADING · EMPTY · ERROR · STALE · SAMPLE
GENERIC STATE TREATMENTS
STATETREATMENT
LoadingPanel geometry and eyebrows render immediately at final size. Figures are replaced by a 1px baseline rule and a mono placeholder dash. No spinners, no shimmer — the chamfered shell is the skeleton.
EmptyState copy in Barlow 700 at 21–26px plus one plain sentence. Never a zero, never an em-dash grid.
ErrorMoney-tinted panel, 3px money left rule, headline in Barlow italic 800, one sentence on what failed and one on what happens next, plus a ghost RETRY button.
Stale dataCyan live dot stops breathing and drops to 35% opacity. The timestamp chip switches to LAST UPDATED 14 MIN AGO in money-soft. Prices dim to 55% and the price-advantage pill is suppressed entirely — a stale advantage is not an advantage.
Sample dataSAMPLE SLATE chip in the top bar plus DEMO DATA beside the live dot. Required on every screen until live credentials are configured.
Partial dataThe affected block renders its NOT YET AVAILABLE panel; siblings are unaffected. Never suppress a whole screen for one missing feed.
WATCH OUT, no concern Block appears with its heading and red edge rule intact, carrying one confident sentence — “Nothing here worries us. The number is the number.” Never padded, never hidden, never softened back into a warning. Integrity Rule 1.

INTEGRITY RULE 1 NEVER MANUFACTURE A CONCERN
WATCH OUT is structurally required to appear. It is not required to be non-empty. A required block creates pressure to fill it, and a fabricated downside is exactly as dishonest as a fabricated edge.
REQUIREMENTDETAIL
May be empty-statedWhen no material concern exists, the block renders a plain confident statement in product voice: “Nothing here worries us. The number is the number.” or “No red flags on this one.”
May never be inventedNever pad the block with a trivial or generic risk to have something in it. Never dress an ordinary fact up as a warning.
May never be hiddenThe block still appears, still carries its heading and its red edge rule. Absence of concern is information; a missing block is not.
Applies toBet Check WATCH OUT · Game Quick View cautioning factors and WHAT COULD BURN YOU · any future block that carries risk.
Copy patternOne sentence, confident, no hedging, no apology. Do not append “but…” to soften it back into a warning.
INTEGRITY RULE 2 NO FAKE SCARCITY
FOUNDING ACCESS is positioned as what it genuinely is. Confidence sells it. A fake clock does not, and a limit that does not exist is a lie with a timer on it.
