# Pricing Bands — Synthesis

Source: `COMPETITOR_MATRIX.csv`, built from `SEGMENT_AI_PREDICTION.md`, `SEGMENT_SHARP_ODDS.md`, `SEGMENT_PROPS_TRACKING.md`. Every number below traces to one of those three docs; anything not stated there is marked UNKNOWN rather than estimated. Confidence tags ([DIRECT]/[3P-REVIEW]/[APP-STORE-DIRECT]/UNVERIFIED-secondary) are inherited from the segment docs, not re-verified here.

## Bands

### FREE
- **Rithmm** — 7-day free trial only, not an ongoing tier (trial-then-paid).
- **PropsBot.ai** — 7-day free trial only.
- **Outlier** — 7-day free trial only, no non-trial free tier.
- **PlayerProps.ai** — limited free tools only (scope undisclosed); no unrestricted trial.
- **BetQL** — "Get Started for Free" is the primary CTA; an actual ongoing free tier (scope UNKNOWN).
- **Unabated** — free/"essentials" tier, then paid add-ons.
- **Props.Cash** — no persistent free tier, 7-day trial.
- **LineMate** — base app free; paywall is "LineMate+" for parlay recommendations.
- **Action Network** — free base app gets live odds, expert picks, personal bet tracking, alerts, bet %, some articles — a genuinely usable free product, not a stub.
- **Pikkit** — core tracking, analytics, and sportsbook sync (BookSync) are entirely free, no card required.
- **Juice Reel** — base tracker free.
- **BettorEdge** — free to join, no deposit required.
- **Betstamp** — the *consumer* tracker app is free; the B2B PRO product is not (see $100+ band).

**What a user gets for $0**: across trackers (Pikkit, Juice Reel, Action Network, BettorEdge, Betstamp-consumer), the free tier includes real sportsbook sync/tracking, not just a demo. Across picks/prediction products (Rithmm, PropsBot.ai, Outlier, PropJuice.ai), "free" is a time-boxed trial, not an ongoing tier — you get the full paid product for 7–14 days, then nothing.

### $10-20 /mo
- **Props.Cash** — $19.99/mo, all-sport player-prop hit-rate tables, rolling-window filters, injuries/weather/lineups context, odds comparison. No AI predictions, no picks.
- **Outlier Premium** — $19.99/mo: trending insights/recommended bets, basic odds tracking, injury reports, alerts.
- **BetQL Premium** — $19.99/mo, 1 sport: computer-model picks, star ratings, public bet%.
- **LineMate** — $14.99/mo (web) or $9.99–$14.99/mo (conflicting App Store SKUs), single premium tier ("LineMate+") for parlay recommendations layered on a free base app.
- **OddsJam "Trends"** — $19.99/mo (App Store add-on tier; narrow scope, not the core EV/arb product).

**What a user gets for $10-20**: single-purpose research or one-sport picks — hit-rate tables, basic trend/odds tracking, or entry-level computer picks. Not the AI-chat, model-builder, or +EV/arb tooling that shows up at higher price points.

### $20-35 /mo
- **Rithmm Core** — $29.99/mo, all sports: AI predictions, prop win-probabilities, spread/ML/total, line shopping, custom model building (Core still gets the builder, though "Scout" AI chat is Pro/Premium+).
- **Outlier Premium+** — $29.99/mo: adds a "Positive EV badge" (DVIG/no-vig math) and per-book line-movement tracker.
- **BetQL Pro/VIP** — $24.99–$29.99/mo, 2–3 sports.
- **Action Network PRO Monthly** — $24.99/mo (or $14.99/week): expert pick alerts, money %, sharp action report, prop projections, custom tracking.

**What a user gets for $20-35**: this is where AI-native picks products with real feature depth (custom models, EV badges) start, and where multi-sport BetQL tiers land. It is the most crowded band in the whole dataset.

### $35-60 /mo
- **Rithmm Pro** — $49.99/mo: adds "Scout" AI chat/reasoning assistant.
- **BetQL Sharp** — $49.99/mo, all sports: "Sharp Picks," public bet%, line-movement tracker, 1-5 star model picks.
- **PropsBot.ai** — $49.99/mo: 0-100 confidence score, Edge Score, line shopping across 25+ books, best-in-segment transparent/timestamped track-record dashboard.
- **PlayerProps.ai** — $59/mo (site) or $59.99/mo (Apple), single all-sport tier: predictions across game markets + props, BetScore ranking, line movement, Discord community, education.
- **OddsJam "Fantasy Optimizer"** — $59.99/mo (a fantasy-pickem add-on, not the core EV product).

**What a user gets for $35-60**: the ceiling of the AI-native prediction category (all four AI-native products with confirmed pricing top out here), plus BetQL's all-sport tier. This is also where an AI chat/reasoning feature (Scout) and the segment's best self-serve transparency tooling (PropsBot.ai's dashboard) both appear.

### $60-100 /mo
- **Rithmm Premium** — $99.99/mo: top tier, deepest model/factor tools.
- **Outlier Pro** — $79.99/mo: full +EV feed, sharp-book comparison, middling tools, arbitrage feed.
- **OddsJam "Gold"** — ~$199/mo per 3P sources but one App Store SKU path (Fantasy Picks $79.00) lands here; Gold itself sits in the $100+ band below.
- **Unabated Props+** — ~$99/mo (UNVERIFIED-secondary).

**What a user gets for $60-100**: full arbitrage/+EV tooling (Outlier Pro) or the top of an AI-prediction tier ladder (Rithmm Premium) — this band is where "AI predictions" and "odds/EV scanning" categories start to overlap in price even though their underlying product is different.

### $100+ /mo
- **OddsJam Gold** — ~$199/mo (3P-sourced); Sharp Money / Platinum $199.99–$499.99/mo (App-Store-direct).
- **Unabated Premium** — ~$199/mo (~$167/mo annual, UNVERIFIED-secondary).
- **RebelBetting Pro** — $209/mo (or $139/mo annual).
- **RebelBetting Starter** — $99/mo — sits right at the edge of this band.

**What a user gets for $100+**: full-scale +EV/arbitrage scanning across many books with real-time alerts (OddsJam Platinum, RebelBetting Pro), or a broader sharp-odds data platform layered with education (Unabated Premium). This band is explicitly aimed at bettors trying to extract EV/arbitrage at volume, not at picks/predictions consumers.

### Professional / hundreds
- **Betstamp PRO** — $249/mo base, +$99/mo PPH feeds, +$129/mo Alts add-on (fully loaded ≈$477/mo); "Props"/"Live" invite-only tiers priced above that on request. This is a B2B data-infrastructure product (SLA, API, enterprise segments — bettor syndicates, sportsbook operators, market makers, DFS operators, media/affiliates, pro team analytics), not a consumer product.
- **Unabated Concierge** — ~$799/mo (~$667/mo annual, UNVERIFIED-secondary).
- **OddsJam Positive EV / Positive EV Global** — $499.99/mo and $399.99/mo respectively (App-Store-direct).

**What a user gets at professional pricing**: enterprise SLAs, sub-second refresh, 200+ book coverage including PPH/offshore/prediction-market feeds, and API access (Betstamp), or the top-ceiling consumer +EV scanning tier (OddsJam, Unabated). No AI-prediction product in this dataset reaches this band — only sharp-odds/data-infrastructure products do.

## Cross-cutting analysis

| Product/tier | Monthly | Annual | Implied monthly-equivalent | Implied discount vs. 12x monthly |

**Norm**: annual discounts cluster in the **~17-34%** range for most products, with two outliers — Action Network's ~60% (its annual figure is a live, directly-verified checkout price, so this is a real, unusually steep annual incentive) and BetQL's Premium tier math, which is inconsistent enough with the rest of BetQL's own ladder that it should be re-verified before being treated as a norm. **A ~20-30% annual discount is the market default; anything richer (Action Network, BetQL's Premium tier) is the exception, not the rule.**

### Trial norms
7 days is the dominant free-trial length (Rithmm, PropsBot.ai, Outlier, Props.Cash, LineMate all confirmed at 7 days). RebelBetting is the outlier at 14 days but caps daily bet volume (50 bets/day) during the trial — a volume cap, not just a time cap. PlayerProps.ai is the clear exception with **no unrestricted trial at all**, substituting a paid $20/7-day week pass instead. Several products (BetQL, OddsJam, Action Network) have unverified or unclear trial terms.

### Week-pass norms
Week/short-duration passes are **rare, not standard** — only two clearly confirmed in this dataset: PlayerProps.ai's $20/7-day pass (paid, not free) and Action Network's $14.99/week PRO tier (a genuine weekly subscription option, not a one-off pass). BetQL's $4.99 "All Sports Day Pass" is a 24-hour pass, a different mechanic again. Juice Reel's marketplace sellers price weekly ($0.96-$14.99/wk) but that's a per-seller price, not a platform pass. **Conclusion: a dedicated short-duration pass is a minority pattern (3 of 17 products, each structured differently) — there is no single "week pass" convention to match, which gives latitude in how (or whether) to offer one.**

### Sport-specific vs. all-sport pricing
**Rare.** Of 17 products, only three tie price to sport count or offer sport-specific add-ons: **BetQL** (1/2/3/all-sport tiers, its defining pricing architecture), **Props.Cash** (optional NFL/NBA Season Passes at $99.99 layered on top of a $19.99/mo all-sport plan), and **Unabated** (sport-specific add-ons like WNBA Projections ~$129/mo and Tennisform ~$55/mo, on top of tiered base plans). The other 14 products — including every other AI-prediction product (PlayerProps.ai, Rithmm, PropsBot.ai, PropJuice.ai) — sell a single all-sport tier and differentiate by feature depth instead. **The dominant market pattern is all-sport-by-default; per-sport pricing is a deliberate minority choice, most visibly BetQL's core architecture.**

### Where the market is thickest and thinnest
- **Thickest: $20-35/mo and $35-60/mo.** Together these two bands hold Rithmm Core/Pro, BetQL's mid tiers, PropsBot.ai, PlayerProps.ai, Outlier Premium+, and Action Network PRO — effectively the entire AI-prediction segment plus several props-tracking/consumer products. Any new entrant priced in $20-60/mo is entering the most contested part of the market.
- **Thinnest: FREE-to-$10 (nothing found priced under $10/mo as an ongoing paid tier — free tiers exist but nothing charges under $10) and the $60-100 band**, which holds only Rithmm Premium and Outlier Pro with confirmed numbers — most of what nominally sits near $60-100 (OddsJam Gold, Unabated Props+) is either above it or unverified. **The professional/hundreds band is thin by design** — only Betstamp, Unabated Concierge, and OddsJam's top EV tiers reach it, and only Betstamp is explicitly B2B/enterprise; it is a real but narrow band that assumes a fundamentally different buyer (syndicates, operators, media/affiliates) rather than a power-user consumer.

## Evaluating Brey's candidate pricing against the evidence

**ONE SPORT: $29.99/mo or $299/yr**
- Lands in the **$20-35/mo band** at $29.99/mo — directly beside **Rithmm Core** ($29.99/mo exactly, all sports) and just above **BetQL's Pro/VIP tiers** ($24.99-$29.99/mo, 2-3 sports) and **Outlier Premium+** ($29.99/mo).
- $299/yr implies ~$24.92/mo effective, a ~17% annual discount — on the low end of the ~20-30% norm found above; consider whether a steeper annual price (nearer $239-259/yr, matching Rithmm Core's ~33% discount) would read as more competitive, or whether a flatter discount is deliberate margin protection.
- **Direct neighbor concern**: Rithmm Core is priced identically ($29.99/mo) and is *all-sport*, not one-sport — a prospect comparing on price alone sees Rithmm covering every sport for the same money we'd charge for one. Our one-sport plan needs to win on something Rithmm doesn't have at that price (see below), not on breadth.
- **What these neighbors offer that a one-sport plan at $29.99 would not**: Rithmm Core bundles all sports, prop win-probabilities, and a genuine model-builder at this exact price; BetQL's neighboring tiers bundle "Sharp Picks" (public-bet%/sharp-money signal) and a 5-min-refresh line-movement tracker that no AI-prediction product in this data offers at any price.
- **What we would offer that they do not** (per the evidence, not asserted as fact about our build): per the customer-pain research, the single clearest, unaddressed gap across every product actually inspected at the code/copy level (Props.Cash, LineMate) is a **complete absence of any sample-size warning or denominator display next to a hit-rate claim** — LineMate structurally builds a feature category around "100% Hit Rate" splits with a 3-game floor. None of Rithmm/BetQL/Outlier/PropsBot.ai/PropJuice.ai publish a third-party-audited track record either. A pricing narrative built on "verifiably not a coin flip" and "we show you the sample size" sits in genuine, evidenced whitespace at this price point — it is not table stakes here, it is differentiated.

**ALL SPORTS: $49.99-59.99/mo or $499/yr**
- Lands squarely in the **$35-60/mo band**, directly beside **Rithmm Pro** ($49.99/mo, adds Scout AI chat), **BetQL Sharp** ($49.99/mo, all-sport, Sharp Picks + line movement), **PropsBot.ai** ($49.99/mo, transparent track-record dashboard), and **PlayerProps.ai** ($59/mo-$59.99/mo, at the very top of the range).
- $499/yr matches **PlayerProps.ai's $499.99/yr almost exactly** — this is the most price-identical neighbor in the entire dataset. PlayerProps.ai at that price bundles game-market predictions, player props, BetScore ranking, a 19,000+-member Discord, and heavy education/community positioning, but has **two industry-award claims with no located methodology and no public ledger found by any reviewer** — a trust gap our evidence base repeatedly flags as unaddressed across the segment.
- **Direct neighbor concern at $49.99/mo specifically**: this is the exact price of both Rithmm Pro (which adds an AI-chat "Scout" feature, the only confirmed AI-chat feature anywhere in the AI-prediction segment) and BetQL Sharp (which adds sharp-money/public-bet-% signals no AI-prediction competitor offers). A prospect at $49.99/mo can already get an AI-chat assistant (Rithmm) or a sharp-money signal (BetQL) for the same money.
- **What these neighbors offer that we do not** (per evidence): Rithmm Pro's "Scout" AI-chat/reasoning assistant (the customer-pain data shows this is genuinely loved — "I just ask scout" replacing multi-site research); BetQL's public-bet-percentage and "Sharp Picks" sharp-money-activity signal; PropsBot.ai's self-serve, date-rangeable, closing-line-referenced track-record dashboard (the most transparent self-audit tool found in the segment).
- **What we would offer that they do not** (per evidence, whitespace not yet built by us): (1) explicit sample-size/denominator display and small-sample warnings — a gap found in every prop tool actually inspected; (2) a third-party-audited (not just self-graded) track record — no product in either the AI-prediction or props-research segments has this; (3) if positioned as a research-teaching copilot rather than a pick-seller, this echoes PlayerProps.ai's most loyal-customer language ("teaches you to think, not a pick machine") without inheriting its unverified-award-claim liability.

**Professional tier (possible)**
- If priced anywhere near the **$100+ or professional/hundreds bands**, the relevant neighbors shift entirely away from consumer AI-prediction products and toward **Betstamp PRO** ($249-$477/mo, B2B data-infrastructure: SLA, 200+ book/feed coverage, API, enterprise buyer segments) and **OddsJam's Positive EV tiers / Unabated Concierge** ($399.99-$799/mo, high-volume +EV/arbitrage scanning for serious/professional bettors).
- **These neighbors offer**: enterprise SLAs, sub-second odds refresh, 200+ sportsbook/PPH/prediction-market feed coverage, and API access (Betstamp) — infrastructure-grade data delivery, not picks or predictions.
- **We would offer that they do not** (if a professional tier were AI-prediction-native rather than a data-feed product): AI-generated predictions/explanations at all — none of Betstamp, OddsJam, or Unabated's top tiers are prediction products; they are odds/EV-scanning and data-infrastructure products. A professional tier priced here would be competing in a different category than "the best AI picks," and should be positioned and evidenced accordingly (this pricing analysis does not have direct evidence for what "professional" would need to include beyond the general market pattern above).
- **Explicitly not a recommendation to compete on lowest price** in any band: the evidence shows customers cancel over perceived-value mismatch and billing/trial deception far more than over sticker price per se (see cancellation-driver clustering in `CUSTOMER_PAIN.md` — deceptive cancellation practices and "picks no better than a coin flip" are the two most repeated complaint themes, ahead of raw price). The differentiation opportunities identified above (sample-size transparency, audited track record, clean cancellation/billing practices) are the evidenced levers, not a lower number.

## Single biggest gap in the evidence

**No product's real-world, audited, pre-registered accuracy or ROI could be independently confirmed anywhere in this research.** Every accuracy/ROI figure found — PropsBot.ai's 27.8% ROI, PropJuice.ai's 65-80% accuracy claims, PlayerProps.ai's award claims, Rithmm's third-party-attributed 72%, BetQL's "10,000 simulations," RebelBetting's EUR23M member-profit figure — is either self-graded with no named external auditor, sourced from an unaudited third party, or entirely undisclosed as to sample size and time window. This is the evidentiary gap the entire competitive set shares, and it is also therefore the single largest open question for **our own** pricing and positioning: the evidence supports "no competitor has a verifiably audited track record" as a differentiation claim, but does not yet establish that we do either — that would need to be true of our own product before it can be used as a pricing or marketing lever.
