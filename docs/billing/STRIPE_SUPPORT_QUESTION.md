# Question for Stripe support (Brey sends; production billing stays off until answered)

Why: Stripe's published restricted-business list names sports forecasting
"with a monetary or material prize". LineHound awards no prize, so it does not
obviously fall under that line, but that is our reading, not Stripe's
approval. Account verification is not approval of the business either. Ask in
writing and keep the answer.

Where: Stripe Dashboard, Support, "Contact support", from the LineHound
account. Paste:

```
Before I take any live payment I want to confirm my business is supported.

LineHound (linehound.app) sells a monthly subscription, $19.99, to sports
betting analysis: written matchup analysis and suggested bets for MLB and
other sports, published before each game and graded in public afterward.

We do not take wagers, we do not hold or move customer funds for betting, we
are not a sportsbook or an affiliate of one, and we award no prizes. We make
no profit or win-rate promises; the site states it is analysis, not advice.

Is this business supported on Stripe in the United States? If it needs
review or extra information, what should I send?
```

Record the date sent and the answer in `docs/billing/PURCHASE_REHEARSAL.md`.
If Stripe says no, we do not switch providers to get around it; we stop and
decide with the answer in hand.
