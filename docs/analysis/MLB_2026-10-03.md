## What the product got wrong today

The card's first pick (Ty France) and the board's largest gaps are all
hitters facing the best starter on the slate. A prop number that ignores
the opposing pitcher is the clearest weakness this exercise shows, and it is
the first thing a baseball-literate tester will notice.
