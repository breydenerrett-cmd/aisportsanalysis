# Loss diagnosis 03 -- what the prices say about us

Research agent 3 of 3, 2026-10-16. Question: closing-line value, timing,
book choice, vig, and how the market reacts to the same information, for the
public MLB card (DAILY_CARD_BEST_BETS_V2), 97-81 and -1.42 units over 178
graded entries since 2026-09-22.

Everything below is generated by one script from local stores only:

    python3 scripts/diagnosis/prices.py          # every section; writes the three files below
    python3 scripts/diagnosis/prices.py --section clv|trajectory|vig|stale|moneyline|baseline|rules   # print one

Outputs (deterministic: two consecutive runs give the same sha256 for the tables file):

- `data/research/loss_diagnosis/prices_tables.md` -- every table in full. This page quotes it; where they differ, the file wins.
- `data/research/loss_diagnosis/prices_entries.jsonl` -- one row per graded entry with every measured field.
- `data/research/loss_diagnosis/prices_summary.json` -- the headline numbers.

Evidence rules held. No outcome from 2026-01-01..2026-08-27 is read: every
outcome reader drops a sealed-date row before it touches any other field.
2025 is not used. Outcomes come only from 2026-08-28 onward and from 2024.
There are no network calls. Line-shopping value is called price
improvement, never edge. Intervals are 95%, date-clustered bootstrap
(`src.model.discovery.clustered_bootstrap`, 2000 resamples, fixed seed,
21 card dates).

## 0. Plain-words conclusions (read this first)

1. **No sign we know something the market does not, at the price we are graded at.**
   Closing-line value (CLV) here is the closing de-vigged consensus minus
   the breakeven of the graded price. It is **-1.69 probability points
   [-2.08, -1.28]** on the 82 of 178 entries that can be measured. The point
   estimate is negative in every segment with at least two measured
   entries. The interval excludes zero everywhere except four small
   segments: plus-money, hits Unders, home moneylines, and games locked
   2-4 h before first pitch. Restricted to closes within 90 minutes of first
   pitch: -0.96 [-1.65, +0.04] (n=29, mostly moneylines).
   Answer to "do we have information the market lacks": **no at the graded
   price. On direction it is unknown, with a small hint** (point 3).

2. **The loss is the price, not the sides.** Every graded price is the best
   two-sided price on the board at the quote (170/170 rebuilt). Even so,
   every entry paid a premium over the de-vigged consensus, because the
   card's own G13 gate refuses any candidate whose consensus already clears
   its best price. The premium averages **+2.36 points per bet** (props
   +2.1 to +3.0, moneylines +1.1). Decomposed:
   - the same 97-81 results at the lock consensus fair price would be **+5.84 units**;
   - the premium cost **-7.26 units**;
   - actual: -1.42.

   The lock market expected these bets to return -7.55 units, and they
   returned -1.42. The +6-unit gap is the size of luck: hit rate minus the
   lock consensus probability is +1.45 points per bet [-5.91, +10.32].

3. **After we bet, the market drifts a little toward our props.** On taken
   props, consensus moves +0.53 points [+0.12, +0.89] toward our side
   between the quote and the last pre-pitch capture. On refused close calls
   it moves -0.21 [-0.43, +0.04]. Taken minus refused: **+0.74 [+0.32, +1.07]**.
   The drift sits in props quoted on the season-average path, before
   lineups are used: +0.70 [+0.21, +1.13]. It is about a quarter of the
   premium paid, so it never turns CLV positive. Moneylines show no clear
   drift (+0.23 [-0.39, +1.05]). This is the only price-side hint of
   information. It is small, and it rests on 56 props measured mostly about
   two hours before first pitch.

4. **"Graded at average odds" is not the V2 rule.** V2 grades at the
   published price, which is the best two-sided price at the quote instant.
   `card_ledger` settlement uses the frozen `price`, and the prop board and
   the moneyline snapshot both take the best decimal. Graded at the board
   average instead, the same 170 entries would be **-4.11 units, not -0.69**.
   Best-price grading already helps by about 3.4 units. In hindsight, the
   best price any book showed between the quote and first pitch would have
   made those 170 +0.50 (retrospective, not takeable).

5. **Stale and thin boards do not explain the loss.**
   - Bets on 1-2 books went 16-5 (+7.96); 3+ books went 81-76 (-9.37).
   - Quotes more than 6 hours old at lock went 33-23 (+3.12); quotes at most 5 minutes old went 33-36 (-8.59).
   - CLV is the same in every bucket within its interval.
   - `stale_board` was never set on any of 175 published rows.
   - `point_class` lives on a different ledger (`decisions_v2.jsonl`, the paper systems) and has one value, LATE_BOARD, on all 5,689 rows.

   Neither field can predict anything.

6. **The 29 moneylines are all favourites, 24 of them at home, all
   MAIN-class.** They won 19-10 (+4.83). CLV was -0.90 [-1.51, -0.08], and
   the consensus moved toward us about as often as against us. The
   moneyline record is outcome luck on a near-fair price, not price skill.

7. **The bar.** The de-vigged consensus barely beats a constant forecast on
   hitter props: Brier skill +2.4% on hits 0.5, +0.9% to +1.2% on total
   bases. It beats a constant by about 4% on moneylines (2024: Brier
   0.2401, n=2456; 2026-08-28 onward: 0.2385, n=345). On the 178 entries we
   chose, our raw number had Brier 0.2569 against the market's 0.2493 (ours
   minus market +0.0076 [-0.0059, +0.0187]). That is not significantly
   worse, and not better. On the side we picked, our number averaged 60.0%,
   against a 54.5% hit rate and a 53.0% market number. The model has to
   beat the market by more than the 2.4-point premium before any price-side
   rule matters.

**What would have changed what (HINDSIGHT, not a result).** No rule built
from books, staleness or timing makes CLV positive. Minimum-book rules make
the record worse (min 4 books: 65-64, -10.94). Only "premium paid at most 2
points" moves CLV toward zero (-0.91 [-1.39, -0.33]). Its +15.2 units on 64
bets is mostly selection on cheap prices, plus noise. Section 7 sets a
forward test for that one rule.

## 1. Closing-line value

**Definitions.**

- `be_lock`: breakeven of the graded price, vig included.
- `p_lock`: the entry's frozen `market_probability`, the de-vigged consensus at the quote.
- `p_close`: de-vigged consensus at the last capture strictly after the quote and strictly before first pitch, on the same selection and line. Props need at least 2 two-sided books (`propboard.MIN_BOOKS`, booksum >= 0.98); moneylines need 6 (`prices.MIN_BOOKS`).
- CLV pts = 100 * (p_close - be_lock); move pts = 100 * (p_close - p_lock); premium pts = 100 * (be_lock - p_lock).

Stores: `data/processed/batter_props.jsonl` for props;
`data/processed/odds_multibook.jsonl` plus its archive segments under
`data/processed/archive/odds_multibook/` for moneylines; event ids from
`data/processed/event_game_map.jsonl`.

**Doubleheaders are refused, not guessed.** On 2026-09-25 BAL@NYY the event
map pinned both events to the same game_pk (`ambiguous: true`). Matching on
nearest commence time then measured one Yankees entry against the wrong
game. So any game_pk named on an ambiguous map row is marked
`AMBIGUOUS_EVENT`. That covers 8 entries: 3 moneylines and 5 props.

**Instrument check.**

- The quote board was rebuilt for 170/178 entries; the 8 missing are the ambiguous ones.
- The graded price equals the rebuilt best two-sided price on 170/170.
- The frozen `market_probability` equals the rebuilt consensus (within 0.0011) on 170/170.
- Two entries were recomputed by hand from the raw rows. Phillies 2026-09-27 moneyline: CLV +3.142 both ways. Nick Sogard total bases 0.5 Over 2026-09-22: closing consensus 0.57851 both ways.

**Measurability.** 82/178 entries (46.1%) have a later quote: moneylines
26/29 (89.7%), **props 56/149 (37.6%)**. Absences: 88 `NO_LATER_QUOTE`, 8
`AMBIGUOUS_EVENT`. Prop captures run at about 04:00Z (baseline) plus one to
three gate captures, the last usually around 22:00Z. A prop locked inside
the final two hours therefore has nothing after it.

**Why the premium is never below zero.** G13 (`best_bets_card.failed_gates`)
refuses a candidate whose de-vigged consensus is above the breakeven of its
best price; the owner ruled against line shopping. So every graded entry is
priced at or worse than the consensus fair price at the quote. On the 178,
the minimum premium is +0.06 points, the median +2.53 and the maximum +3.96.
Every bet starts about 2.4 points under water by construction, before the
closing market moves at all.

**How much of the loss is vig and how much is wrong sides.** Across the card,
the premium accounts for all of the loss and more. The sides ran 2.6 wins
ahead of the lock market (+1.45 points per bet, interval [-5.91, +10.32]),
which cannot be told apart from zero. By market:

- **Hits:** lost nothing on sides (+4.53 at fair) and gave back 2.58 in premium.
- **Total bases lost on both.** Sides cost -2.13 (41-37, against a market expecting about 42 wins). Premium cost -4.06, the largest of any market (3.0 points per bet).
- **Moneylines:** won on sides (+5.45, luck-sized) and paid little premium.

Grading at average odds would add about 3.4 units of loss. That rule is not
in force for V2, so it explains none of the current loss.

Thin and stale entries did not lose; if anything they won, which is noise at
these counts. CLV does not separate them. The best book's own
`book_last_update` was within 30 minutes of the capture on all 170 rebuilt
quotes, so stale *book feeds* are not the issue either.

**Was the graded price still there at lock?**

- **Props.** No capture of any graded prop exists between its quote and its
  `locked_at`, so the store cannot say whether the price had moved. 42 props
  were frozen on a quote more than 6 hours old (the 04:00Z baseline), and the
  card locked them on that quote. For the 41 props with a quote over an hour
  old and a capture after the lock, grading at that capture's best price
  gives -1.78, against -1.28 actual.
- **Moneylines.** 20/29 had a newer multibook capture before `locked_at`.
  - The graded price was still the best available on only 9/20; mean -3.0 cents.
  - Worst case: 2026-10-04 Dodgers, graded -147 with -212 the best at lock (a loss).
  - Re-graded at the lock-time best price, those 20 go from +3.01 to +3.17 units.

  So the stale moneyline quotes did not flatter the record in total. But the
  card is grading moneyline fills at a morning price that a reader could no
  longer get. Whoever owns the card plumbing should look at this; it is
  outside this task's boundary, and nothing was edited.

`stale_board` is false or absent on all 175 published rows. `point_class` is
`LATE_BOARD` on all 5,689 rows of `evidence/decisions_v2.jsonl`. That file
is the paper-system ledger (market-derived and engine systems), not the
card, and it has no join key to card entries. Neither field can predict
losses on this record.

**Forward test (premium cap only; the other rules already fail on price).**

- **Rule.** Pre-register a shadow rule before 2026-10-17: a graded entry is kept if 100 * (breakeven(price) - market_probability) <= 2.0.
- **Population.** Every V2 entry graded after registration.
- **Primary metric.** Mean CLV against the last pre-pitch capture, with a date-clustered 95% interval. Success: the interval is above zero at n >= 150 measured entries.
- **Secondary metric.** CLV difference against the complement; success if its interval excludes zero.
- **Units** are reported but are not the test.
- **Precondition.** The test needs prop captures inside the last hour before first pitch. Without them, about 60% of props stay unmeasurable and the test cannot finish.

## What could not be measured

- **CLV for 93 of 149 props (62%).** There is no capture after the quote and before first pitch. These are the late-locked, lineup-known picks, and they hold most of the prop loss. The measured subset is early, fill-heavy and winning, so the prop CLV of -2.06 does not describe the losing bets.
- **A true prop close.** The last prop capture is a median 110 minutes before first pitch, and never closer than 49.
- **The price a reader could get when a prop was frozen.** No capture lies between a prop's quote and its lock.
- **Book choice as the card states it.** `book` is null on every graded entry, so the book is rebuilt from the quote board.
- **Pre-quote drift on props.** The graded quote is the selection's first capture on 100 of the 144 props with an unambiguous event.
- **Doubleheader entries** (8, `AMBIGUOUS_EVENT`). They are refused rather than matched by guesswork.
- **point_class and stale_board as predictors.** Each has one value on its ledger and does not join to card entries.
- **A 2024 prop baseline.** No 2024 prop prices exist locally.
