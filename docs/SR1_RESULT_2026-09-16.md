# SR1 home underdog price band — RUN, KILLED, no edge

**Verdict, first line: this idea did not work, and it is dead. Betting the home
team when the market prices it as a modest underdog did not beat the market's
own fair price in either price band, so nothing here may be bet, sold, shown to
a customer, or described as an edge.**

Two further things a reader outside the project should take away before any
detail:

1. **This was never a fair test of whether the idea is true.** The sample is far
   too small to detect the effect that was pre-registered as worth having. A
   "no" here means "this sample could not see it," not "the effect does not
   exist." Every number below carries that sentence.
2. **These are discovery seasons (2023 and 2024), which are diagnostic and are
   never evidence.** The best outcome this run could ever have produced was
   "worth forward-testing." It did not produce that. The worst outcome was
   "killed." That is what it produced.

The run was executed against `docs/PREREG_SR1_HOME_UNDERDOG.md` (amendment 1),
frozen and registered earlier the same day. Nothing was changed between freeze
and run: the freeze hash recomputes to
`07b5c9728e6efbf818e24196b7f565ca4c962f09770886c0c367635aa15e0a37` and matches
the amendment row on the registry ledger exactly. The run was then independently
recomputed end to end by a second, separate implementation, which reproduced
every published number to the published decimal and agreed with the kill.

Sections 1 to 3 below are preserved verbatim from the earlier version of this
document, because they are the finding that produced the registration in the
first place. Everything from §4 onward is new and supersedes the earlier
"registered, attempted, NOT EVALUATED" text: the blocking coverage hole that
stopped the first attempt (no 2023/2024 outcome store) was closed by a backfill
through `src.pipeline.history.ingest_range` from the free MLB StatsAPI, and the
screen then ran.

## 5. Method, as registered

1. Read the season odds archive; drop any snapshot at or after that event's own
   `commence_time`.
2. Take the latest remaining snapshot **at least 360 minutes** before that
   event's first pitch. That single snapshot is the decision instant.
3. Require **at least 6 books** at that instant. Compute the consensus home
   American price and the proportionally de-vigged consensus home probability
   from that snapshot and that book set only.
4. Assign band membership on the rounded consensus home American price, home
   side only, edges inclusive: **[100, 150] = band A**, **[151, 250] = band B**.
5. Grade: `won` from `pricepath.read_results`; statistic = mean(won − de-vigged
   implied); decision p = max(p_date, p_team) and decision CI = the union of the
   date-clustered and home-team-clustered 95% percentile bootstrap intervals
   (2,000 resamples, seed 20260828); then the sign gate, the 2024 replication,
   BH at q = 0.10 over the family of exactly two, then the battery.

No threshold, band, snapshot rule, cluster key or seed was changed at any point.

**A correction the runner owes the record, found by the verifier:** the runner's
first write-up described the 543→528 delta as a departure from a "counting

the explanation attached to it was wrong and is corrected here.

**A limitation that cannot be fixed on the registered code path:** §11 requires

`pricepath.read_results` drops undecided rows before the join, so an undecided

## 9. Power — read this before reading anything above as "no effect"

**UNDERPOWERED_NULL.** This sample can only rule out effects above about **6
percentage points in band A** and **11 to 12 percentage points in band B** (the
pre-registered date-axis minimum detectable effects at 80% power; on the binding
home-team axis the bars are worse still, roughly 8 to 10 pp in band A and 13 to
16 pp in band B). The registered effect floor was **+1 pp**. A null here is
therefore an UNDERPOWERED_NULL and is **never** a statement that no effect
exists. Detecting a real +1 pp effect would need roughly 19,000 to 54,000
selections per season depending on the axis; band A had 528 and band B 138.

What the realised intervals do rule out, at 95% confidence, keyed to the
realised upper bound and never to the MDE:

- Band A 2023: effects above **+0.58 pp** are ruled out.
- Band B 2023: effects above **+21.97 pp** are ruled out.
- Band B 2024: effects above **+6.40 pp** are ruled out.

And a profitability note that applies to all of it: breakeven at the consensus
price is 1.30 to 1.66 pp above the de-vigged consensus in every cell, so even an
effect exactly at the registered +1 pp floor would be a losing bet at that
price. Clearing the floor is a calibration statement. It is not an edge.

## 10. What this licenses

**May be done:**

- Record SR1 as killed on the alpha registry and in the strategy-replication
  lane's status table, and cite it as a spent, closed unit in future
  multiplicity accounting.
- Reuse the *apparatus* built here — the point-in-time snapshot selector, the
  6-book proportional de-vigged consensus, the date/home-team clustered
  envelope, the outcome-blind join audit and additive reconciliation — for other
  registered families. The machinery was verified; only the hypothesis died.
- Cite the power finding when designing any future price-band family: an MLB
  season yields a few hundred selections in a narrow band, which is one to two
  orders of magnitude short of what a 1 pp effect needs. Design around that or
  do not register it.
- Cite the 2023→2024 apparatus shift (16.4 → 11.2 books) as a known constraint
  on any two-season replication run on these archives.

**May NOT be done:**

- **Nothing here may reach a customer surface.** No pick, card, page, alert,
  export, model input or marketing claim may reference SR1, the home-underdog
  band, or any number in this document.
- No edge may be claimed, in either direction. Not "home underdogs are
  overpriced," not "home underdogs are underpriced," not "the effect does not
  exist."
- Band B 2023's +7.77 pp may not be quoted on its own, promoted, revisited,
  forward-tested, or described as promising. It failed replication, and it is
  below breakeven even at face value.
- No rescue by threshold change. The bands, the 360-minute rule, the 6-book
  floor, the +1 pp floor, the q, the seed and the cluster keys may not be
  altered to produce a survivor from this data. A differently-banded or
  differently-timed version of the same mechanism is a new registration on new
  data, not a re-read of this one.
- SR2, the divisional slice, may not now be computed on 2023/2024 to see whether
  it would have worked. It was deliberately withdrawn before the read precisely
  so no unpromotable number could be seen and later re-read.
- **Discovery data is never evidence.** 2023 and 2024 are diagnostic. The best
  available outcome from this run was "worth forward-testing"; it was not
  reached. Nothing in this document is evidence for or against anything.
- The falsification battery did not run. Its absence may never be presented as a
  pass.
- 2025 remains tuning-only and unspent. The 2026-01-01 to 2026-08-27 sealed
  window remains sealed and unspent by this run.
