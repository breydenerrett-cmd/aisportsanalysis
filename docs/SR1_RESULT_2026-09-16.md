# SR1/SR2 home underdog price band — registered, attempted, NOT EVALUATED

**Verdict, first line: the run did not execute. No outcome row was graded, no
effect, no interval, no p-value and no FDR decision exists.** SR1 was drafted
but unregistered on 2026-09-16; it was registered properly that same day
(`docs/PREREG_SR1_HOME_UNDERDOG.md`, two rows in
`data/research/alpha_registry.jsonl`); the run then stopped because the outcome
store the frozen spec names does not exist for 2023 or 2024. This is a
missing-precondition stop. It is **not** a null, **not** an underpowered
result, and **not** any verdict on whether home underdogs at +100 to +250 carry
value. Nothing here licenses a claim in either direction.

Two further pieces of bad news, stated up front:

1. The run's own written reason for stopping was **factually wrong**. It
   reported that no 2023/2024 game results exist anywhere on disk. They do, in
   `evidence/`. The runner searched `data/` and then reported an on-disk
   conclusion. The stop is still correct, but for a different reason (§8).
2. Two files under `data/research/evolab/` publish **aggregate 2024 outcome
   facts about the exact window SR1 tests**. Nothing in this registration used
   them, and that was verified by independent recomputation, but they are a
   standing contamination surface for whoever runs this next (§8).

This document supersedes nothing in the original check; that check is preserved
verbatim in §1 below, because it is the finding that produced the registration.

>

> read.
>
> **Market.** MLB moneyline (h2h), full game.

> **Sample gate.** Minimum 30 qualifying selections per season per
> sub-hypothesis (consistent with N8's floor rule) ...
>
> **Effect floor.** +1pp over de-vigged consensus implied probability

> agree in sign; BH-FDR q=0.10 applied over this family jointly with
> Template B (and Template C if drafted).

> concentration, book concentration, season-split stability, dose-response

> **Pre-registered prior, stated now.** MEDIUM-LOW ... Recorded before any

## 3. The registration in brief

Full text in `docs/PREREG_SR1_HOME_UNDERDOG.md`. The essentials:

- **Family as registered: two price bands, not band-plus-divisional.**
  Band A = rounded consensus home American price in **[100, 150]** (SR1 as
  drafted). Band B = **[151, 250]**. The widening toward the owner's spoken
  "+100 to +250" was done before any read, disclosed in §1 of the spec, and
  paid for with BH over two hypotheses rather than one.
- **SR2, the divisional slice, is NOT COMPUTED AT ALL.** Its report-only
  stratum was withdrawn so that no unpromotable number could be seen and later
  re-read. Anyone comparing this write-up to the original Template A shortlist
  is comparing different objects; that is deliberate and recorded.
- Direction: back the home moneyline. Market: MLB h2h, full game.
- Decision instant: the latest snapshot at least **360 minutes** before that
  event's own first pitch. Minimum **6** books. Proportional de-vig.
- Effect floor **+1pp** over the de-vigged consensus; **q = 0.10** BH over the
  full registered family; sample gate **30** selections per band per season;
  battery RULES_VERSION 2.0.0, fingerprint `ac74c7a7f715f9ec`; bootstrap 2000
  resamples, seed 20260828.
- Amendment 1 replaced the date-only clustered interval with a date/home-team
  envelope: decision p = max(p_date, p_team), decision CI = union of the two.
  This is strictly conservative and cannot manufacture a survivor.
- Prior: MEDIUM-LOW, recorded before any look.

## 5. Method

Selection is rebuilt from prices alone, using the repo's own point-in-time
conventions:

1. Read the season odds archive. Drop any snapshot at or after
   `commence_time` at read time (1,792 rows dropped in 2023, 1,699 in 2024).
2. For each event, take the latest remaining snapshot at least 360 minutes
   before that event's own first pitch. That single snapshot is the decision
   instant.
3. Require at least 6 books at that instant. Compute the consensus home
   American price and the proportionally de-vigged consensus home probability
   from that snapshot only.
4. Assign band membership on the rounded consensus home American price, home
   side only, edges inclusive: [100, 150] band A, [151, 250] band B.
5. Grade: `won` from the repo's own result store via `pricepath.read_results`,
   `diff` = realised win rate minus de-vigged consensus probability, decision p
   and CI as the date/home-team envelope, BH at q = 0.10 over the family, then
   the falsification battery.

Steps 1 through 4 ran. **Step 5 did not run at all**, because
`pricepath.read_results` has nothing to return for 2023 or 2024.

Two things that table says out loud and should not be skimmed past. First, the
**+1pp effect floor is far below what this sample can resolve**: the minimum
detectable effect at 80% power is 6 to 12 percentage points, and resolving a
genuine +1pp edge would need roughly 18,000 to 19,000 selections per cell

almost no information. Second, **band B is heavily concentrated**: five home
teams account for 75% and 80% of its selections, which the battery's team
concentration rule is there to catch.

## 8. Discrepancies and hazards found

Published because the rule is publish every loser, including every flaw in the
work itself. None of these change a band count.

1. **The run's stated reason for stopping is false.** It claimed no 2023/2024
   results exist anywhere on disk. `evidence/archive_2026-08-28/selections_2023_24.json`
   (7,420 rows) and `evidence/stage2_2026-08-28/selections.json` (13,324 rows)
   carry per-game outcomes for 4,374 distinct 2023/24 `game_pk` with zero
   contradictions between them. The runner scoped its search to `data/` and
   reported an on-disk conclusion, then closed by asking to be pointed at a
   source its own repo was holding.
2. **The stop is still correct, for a different reason.** §4 of the frozen spec
   fixes the outcome source as the repo's own result store via
   `pricepath.read_results`, and §11 names the StatsAPI backfill into
   `data/historical/mlb_results.csv` as the only sanctioned fix. The
   `evidence/` files are a different provenance: derivatives of an earlier
   graded run, over a game set conditioned on what some detector selected,
   about 90% of the 2023/24 schedule. Substituting them would be a definition
   change after freeze, which voids the run under §16 rule 8. The verifier
   therefore computed no diff from them, deliberately, and neither should
   anyone else. The honest statement is "the conformant outcome store is
   absent", not "no outcome data exists".
3. **Contamination surface, live and unused.**
   `data/research/evolab/market_derived_calibration_2024.json` publishes the
   2024 observed home-win rate 0.5255147717099373 over n = 2,234, and
   `data/research/evolab/phase2a_baseline.json` records a 2023-train /
   2024-eval fit with zero no-result rows in both seasons. These are aggregate
   outcome facts about the exact window SR1 tests, sitting on disk and readable
   before the read. Independent recomputation found no sign the registration
   used them, since every §4/§9/§10 figure comes out identical from prices
   alone. They remain a hazard for the next runner.
4. **A leakage hazard inside the evidence files.** Both carry `closing_implied`
   (a price after the decision instant) alongside `won`, and their game set is
   detector-conditioned. SR1 uses neither, but if anyone later builds the
   outcome join from these files, the price field is a leakage hazard and the
   conditioned sample is a selection-bias hazard the spec's 10% join-loss test
   would not catch. For the record, the verifier's join loss against that
   reconstruction was 3.7 / 3.5 / 3.2 / 5.1 per cent, inside the 10% kill line,
   which means the kill line would have passed a source that should not be
   used.
5. **§9 reconciliation row is wrong.** It reports zero team names the odds
   store could not spell out. There are two per season, "National League" and
   "American League", belonging to exactly one event each: the All-Star Game
   (2023-07-12, 2024-07-17). They are correctly dropped and no band is
   affected, but the figure should read 1 and 1, not 0 and 0. That row exists
   specifically to attest join health.
6. **§9 event accounting is internally inconsistent about that same event.**
   Independent counts give 2,474 and 2,471 events seen against the published
   2,475 and 2,472, which count the All-Star event. For 2023 the published
   "events priced" 2,198 excludes it; for 2024 the published 2,194 includes it
   against an independent 2,193. Same event, two treatments across two season
   columns. Off by one, no band count changes.
7. **The decision-grade cluster key was chosen using sample statistics from
   the data under test.** Amendment 1 §5 justifies the date/home-team envelope
   by citing realised selection-per-date and selection-per-team figures. No
   outcome was read, which is verifiable because no conformant outcome store
   exists, but the statistical key moved after the prices were seen. The
   direction is strictly conservative and the spec discloses it. Recorded as an
   observation, not a violation.
8. **The parent registry row still describes the superseded design.** Row 88's
   note says date-clustered bootstrap over dates, and calls SR2 report-only.
   Amendment 1 changed both. The ledger is append-only by design and row 89
   carries the correction, but a reader querying the registry for this
   hypothesis without reading row 89 gets the wrong cluster key and the wrong
   SR2 status.
9. **Stale brief inherited from DEBRIEF.md line 140**, as recorded in §4.

## 10. What this licenses

Plainly, and with nothing hedged:

- **This licenses no claim of an edge.** There is no measured edge here, in
  either direction, at any price band. Nothing in this document may be shown to
  a customer, quoted in an offer, or used to size a bet.
- **This is not a null.** A null would mean the test ran and found nothing. The
  test did not run. Saying "we tested home underdogs and found nothing" would
  be as false as claiming a win.
- **Discovery data is diagnostic and never evidence.** 2023 and 2024 are the
  discovery window. Even a clean pass on both seasons would license exactly one
  sentence, "worth forward-testing", and would never be a customer claim and
  never a promotion. That ceiling applies to whatever this run eventually
  returns.
- **2025 stays unspent and the sealed window stays shut.** Nothing here changes
  either. The sealed 2026-01-01 to 2026-08-27 window still has its one
  evaluation available, only after a policy freeze and Brey's explicit go.
- **What a forward test would have to show.** Forward proof runs on 2026-08-28
  onward, never folded back into tuning. To mean anything it would need, at
  minimum: selections generated live at the registered decision instant with no
  hindsight in the price; the pre-registered +1pp floor cleared with the
  date/home-team envelope excluding zero; sign agreement with the discovery
  window; survival of the full falsification battery, especially the team
  concentration rule given band B's 75 to 80 per cent top-five concentration;
  and BH-FDR survival across the whole pre-registered family, not the band that
  happens to look best. The power table in §6 says honestly that a real +1pp
  edge needs on the order of 18,000 selections per cell to resolve, which is
  many seasons of forward data, not one. Anyone promising a faster answer is
  changing the bar.
- **No rescue by threshold change.** The bands, the floor, q, the gate, the
  lead time and the book minimum are frozen. If the eventual result fails, it
  fails, and it gets published as a loser.
