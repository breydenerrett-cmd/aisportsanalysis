# Evolution Lab — Phase 2B results

**Verdict: `BELOW_PLACEBO_CEILING`. Evolution does not get built.**

Artifact: `data/research/evolab/sweep-0014914df78666b9-REAL.json` (schema
`evolab.sweep/1`). Every number below is quoted from it.

This is the strongest null this project has produced. It was pre-registered as
the likely outcome (design §14b), it was measured by instruments built to
distrust themselves, and it survived a genuine disagreement between two of
those instruments. Zero survivors is a result.

### Corroboration 1 — PBO

CSCV over 10 chronological blocks, C(10,5) = 252 balanced splits:

- **PBO = 0.6111** — the in-sample winner lands below the out-of-sample median
  in 61% of splits. Above 0.5 means in-sample selection *anti-predicts*
  out-of-sample rank; picking the backtest champion is worse than picking at

### Corroboration 2 — P4 dispersion

Resampled real-world maxima: min 0.006088, median 0.007472, max 0.008827 —
**every one of them above the real 0.004882**. The actual sequence's best
strategy is unremarkable even against resamplings of itself.

## 2. The instrument disagreement, and its adjudication

This is the scientifically interesting part of the run, and it is published in
full because the machinery caught it on the first live number it ever produced.

### What disagreed

Hansen's SPA (consistent variant, 1,000 stationary-bootstrap replications,
block length 7.0, seed 20260831, 365 periods) **rejected its null**:

- statistic (best studentized) 4.7106 on strategy `050046ef26ec2da3`
- p = 0.002997, bounds [0.001998, 0.002997]

The placebo ceiling said noise. The sweep driver's own cross-check refused to
let either number be quoted:

> `spa_cross_check.status = "DISAGREE"` — "SPA rejects but the real maximum
> does not clear the placebo ceiling (p=0.0030, bounds [0.0020, 0.0030]). One
> of the two is wrong: either the placebo worlds are easier than reality, or
> the differential series is not the series the search maximised. **Find the
> bug before quoting either number.**"

Note also that SPA's best strategy (`050046ef26ec2da3`) is not the ceiling's
champion (`4f1b92d3a4cf55ba`) — studentised and raw-mean criteria disagree on
which genome is best, which is itself what a flat, noise-dominated landscape
looks like.

### The discriminating measurement

Measured on the real feed, unconditionally, over all 4,188 games — with no
strategy, no feature, and no selection involved:

| quantity | mean | sd | n | t |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| early→late board movement toward **home** | +0.00154 | 0.0152 | 4,188 | **+6.57** |
| early→late board movement toward the **decision-time favorite** | +0.00073 | — | 4,188 | **+3.08** |

### Diagnosis

There is no bug. The two instruments are answering different questions, and
the drift above is what separates them.

- **SPA benchmarks against zero.** Its null is "no strategy beats a
  do-nothing benchmark of 0". Every strategy in the space inherits the same
  board-wide drift, so the *best* of 8,811 strategies rejects that null on a
  quantity every strategy shares. SPA is working correctly and is being asked
  a question whose answer is "yes, the board drifts".
- **The placebo worlds preserve that drift by construction.** P6 nulls
  *feature-conditional* movement only — it permutes each game's
  (fair-at-decision → fair-at-close) movement pair within date, leaving the
  per-date movement distribution exactly intact. Design §7 warned of this in
  advance, in the note written for this run: *"remember P6 nulls
  feature-conditional movement, not unconditional drift."* P2 and P3 permute
  team identity and shift feature dates respectively, leaving each game's own
  movement attached to that game. All three therefore carry the +0.00154 drift
  into the null world unchanged.
- **Therefore the ceiling reports what it was built to report**: whether the
  search finds anything *beyond* the shared drift. It finds nothing — indeed
  less than the nulls find.

For the lab's actual question — *is there exploitable feature-conditional
signal in this space?* — **the placebo ceiling embodies the correct null and
SPA does not.** The verdict stands unchanged.

The cross-check itself is vindicated: it did not resolve the disagreement, it
refused to let a number be published before someone did. That is exactly its
job, and it did it on the first live run.

## 3. The drift itself — an observation, not an edge

The +0.00154 home-ward movement is real, sizeable in t (+6.57), and **not a
finding this project is registering, claiming, or betting.**

What it is: a ~0.15 percentage-point mean shift in de-vigged **market-implied
consensus** toward the home side between two board snapshots a median 534
minutes (~9 hours) apart at the decision end, with the late board a median
84.35 minutes before first pitch. It is a microstructure observation about our
own two-snapshot store.

Measurement provenance:
`data/research/evolab/drift-measurement-20260831.json` — reproduced
deterministically from the replay feed after this document was drafted; the
numbers match those quoted here to the digit (mean +0.001542, sd 0.015184,
t +6.571; toward-favorite +0.000727, t +3.084; mean |move| 0.010547; n 4,188).

Mundane causes that are fully consistent with it, none of which is
distinguishable from a 3-snapshot-per-day store:

1. **Book-composition change** between the two consensus snapshots — the set of
   books that clears `min_books = 6` and enters the de-vigged consensus is not
   guaranteed identical at the early and late instants, and a change in the
   panel moves the average without anything moving in the market.
2. **De-vig structure differences** — the vig split between sides need not be
   symmetric, and its removal at two different panels/price levels can bias the
   difference.
3. **Genuine line drift** — money arriving between the boards.

Further limits that make it unbettable as stated:

- The **LATE_BOARD is not the close.** Definition: "the last observation before
  first pitch. NOT a close: median 85 minutes out, and every decision point
  carries its own gap_minutes." Distribution over 4,188 rows: min 4.35, median
  84.35, max 359.38 minutes. "De-vigged consensus movement between two boards
  hours apart" is the claim; "movement to the close" is not.
- The consensus price is **not a takeable price** (see §5). Nothing computed
  from it is EV or edge.
- This measurement was made *after* seeing the sweep, on a store whose outcomes
  are known. It is not pre-registered and it carries no multiplicity control.

**No claim is registered here.** If the drift deserves pursuit it goes through
the front door: a pre-registered family evaluated with the frozen machinery
(ceiling, PBO, FDR over the full family), or the V3 forward lane where the
forward ledger arbitrates. It does not get promoted by having turned up as a
side effect of a null.

## 4. What the run proves about the machinery

The verdict is the deliverable, but the day's arc is the durable asset. Three
specification errors and one subtlety were caught by instruments built to
distrust themselves — all before a single real number was published.

1. **P4 was reclassified.** It copies whole game-days with features, prices and
   outcomes still attached, so a planted edge rides into the "null" intact
   (measured: P4 maxima centred 0.230 against a real 0.235 — the one generator
   a planted edge failed to clear). It is now a dispersion diagnostic, excluded
   from the ceiling and the kill criterion. The per-generator kill criterion
   was designed precisely so one mis-specified world could not poison the
   verdict; on this run it earned its keep.
2. **P1 and P5 were exposed as structurally uninformative for the primary
   fitness.** Both permute or resample OUTCOMES only, while movement fitness
   reads prices and features neither touches. The artifact's warnings say it
   plainly: *"every placebo maximum under this generator exactly equals the
   real maximum; [it] changed nothing this fitness depends on … its 'does not
   clear' verdict is a tie, not evidence of a ceiling."* Confirmed in the data:
   all ten P1 and all ten P5 maxima are bit-identical to 0.004882213449032019.
   They remain valid nulls for the outcome-ROI confirmation fitness.
3. **P6 was built and validated** as the movement analogue of P1 — within-date
   permutation of each game's movement pair, features left attached — and
   passed the same faithfulness bar before any number it produced was reported.
4. **The cross-check caught a real subtlety on the first live run** and
   correctly demanded adjudication rather than a headline.

Honest limits on the machinery, published rather than buried:

- **Only 3 of 5 designed generators voted.** Artifact warning, verbatim: *"only
  3 of the five design generators reported; the majority in the kill criterion
  is over what ran, not over what was designed."* The majority is 3-of-3 over
  the generators that actually null this fitness, which is the right set — but
  it is a smaller set than §7 originally specified.
- **Search-size drift across placebo worlds.** 20 warnings record replicates
  whose eligible-strategy count differed from the real 8,811 (P2 range
  8,734–8,843; P4 range 8,700–8,947), each flagged with *"the ceiling is only a
  ceiling when the same search ran on both sides."* The differences are ≤1.5%
  and do not move the verdict — the real max is below the placebo *median*, not
  scraping a threshold — but they are recorded, not smoothed.
- **Starter identity and lineup timing are declared parameters, not
  point-in-time.** `starter_identity = actual_at_first_pitch`: the stored
  probable agrees with the actual starter 99.90% (2023) / 99.92% (2024), "12–41×
  too clean to be a pre-game announcement snapshot, so late scratches are
  invisible"; ~2–8% of rows may carry a starter the live system could not have
  known. Not repairable — no archived probables feed with fetch timestamps
  exists for 2023–24. `lineup_posting = assumed_T_minus_180_minutes`: the
  lineup store records what was posted, not when; under that assumption the
  executable universe falls to ~3,624 games, selected by first-pitch time,
  which correlates with coast and day of week.
- **Nothing in the lab is evidence.** Manifest, verbatim: *"Evolution Lab
  replay — exploratory sandbox, NOT evidence. Replayed against a store whose
  outcomes are already known; only the forward ledger arbitrates. Price
  improvement here is line-shopping value, never EV and never edge."*

## 6. Consequence, and what happens next

The kill criterion (design §15) fires. Its consequence, verbatim from the
artifact: **evolution does not get built.** No elites, parent pools, mutation,
crossover, immigrants, islands, lineage or meta-learning. Searching harder in a
space with nothing in it only manufactures better-looking artifacts.

This was the pre-registered expectation. §14b, recorded before the sweep ran:
Phase 2A had already answered the linear version of the question (+0.0000412
log-loss/game *worse* than the close, date-clustered p = 0.914, L1 selecting the
empty model), and the enumerated space is built from those same features, so
"the enumerable space will most likely land BELOW_PLACEBO_CEILING, and the
lab's expected output is a strong, quantified null rather than a champion." It
did, and it is.

What survives, per §15:

- **The replay engine is a permanent instrument** — point-in-time, outcome-
  isolated, fully fingerprinted — foundational for F5, totals, props,
  microstructure, execution-policy research, future feature families, other
  sports, and forward replay.
- **The noise-ceiling harness is a permanent instrument.** Knowing how much
  apparent edge our own search manufactures from nothing is the deliverable
  regardless of the verdict, and every future family now gets measured against
  it.

Research shifts to: **F5**, **props feasibility**, and **V3 timing and forward
evidence**. Any future look at the board drift of §3 enters through one of
those lanes, pre-registered, or not at all.

## Scope footnote (added 2026-09-02, orchestrator audit)

The genome schema names two markets (`h2h`, `h2h_1st_5_innings`), but
`src/evolab/feed.py` sources full-game h2h prices only; no F5 price ever
reached a Phase 2B genome. The 8,811-strategy headline is therefore an
**h2h-only** search of the 2023–24 board. This does not change the
verdict (BELOW_PLACEBO_CEILING) — it bounds what the verdict covers.
F5 enters the sweep only when Evolab v2 wires an F5 feed.
