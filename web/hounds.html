How to read this table
Seasons
A season is a window of dates. Season 2 started on Oct 18 and every model starts it at zero. Records from earlier are in Season 1 and All-time; nothing is deleted. The figures in this table cover the season you pick. The status column is the referee's verdict and stays all-time.
Ranked, warming up
A model is ranked once it has 10 settled bets in the season shown. Until then it sits in the Warming up group with its record so far, because a 1-0 start tells you nothing.
Units, ROI
One unit a bet, settled at the average price across books when the bet was placed. ROI is units divided by bets.
CLV
How often the price taken beat the price the market closed at, and by how many points on average, over the bets that have a measurable close. n is the number of bets it rests on.
Status
Whether the prices a model takes beat the closing line, judged only once it has 150 measurable bets. It is not a ranking by units, and a model can lead on units while paying the vig.
Last 10
The last ten decided bets, oldest on the left. Green is a win, red a loss, grey a push.
