How the hounds learn

A hound changes only when a test written in advance says a challenger priced better than it did against the closing line. A model can propose a challenger. A model can never promote one. And the loop stops itself if it cannot show it is helping.

Paper only. Not advice. No real bet is ever placed, nothing here tells you what to bet, and a hound's results do not predict anything. 21+.

What has happened, and the false-discovery budget

Many challengers are tested at once, so one that looks good by luck is expected. Each month's finished tests are put through a Benjamini-Hochberg control, there are never more than three promotions a month, and the running total of how many promotions could be flukes is below.
