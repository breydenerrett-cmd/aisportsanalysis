Training camp

Where dogs get better: fire them, ask them, rate their calls, replay their rules.

How this works

Where dogs get better. Fire a dog on the latest boards, ask it why it did what it did, rate its calls, and replay its rule over past days. Dogs don't get edited: rookies try out for the starting job, a rookie makes the team only when the numbers say it beat the starter on the same games, and a fixed rulebook does the cutting, never an AI's hunch. If camp can't show it's helping, it shuts itself down.

Coach report card: is the coach any good?

Once a week the coach suggests one dial tweak. Code grades every tip. A verdict needs 20 settled bets.

How this works

Once a week the coach tells a dog to tweak one of its dials. We don't take its word for it. Code grades every tip two ways. Hindsight replays the 30 days before the tip with the old dial and the new one. The coach could see that stretch, so it's a rehearsal. Live is the honest one: from the minute the tip lands, both dials run side by side on new games. A verdict needs 20 settled bets. Until then it's TOO EARLY.

What's gone down in camp

Camp checks each month for flukes and never promotes more than three rookies.

How this works

Lots of rookies try out at once, so one looks good by pure luck every now and then. Camp keeps a fluke check on each month's finished tryouts, never lets more than three rookies make the team in a month, and keeps a running count of how many of them could be flukes.
