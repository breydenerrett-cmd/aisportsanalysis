    How it works, in three steps.

    1. 1

      Build a hound.

      Say a strategy in plain English or set the rules, or start from one of the house pack. It becomes a hound with a written rule.

    2. 2

      It bets every sport.

      Tennis, cricket, soccer, NBA, NHL, college, MLB, NFL, MMA and more. Each ticket is one unit of paper money, written down before the game. No real money is ever staked.

    3. 3

      We grade it in public.

      Every ticket is marked won or lost once the game is final. Wins and losses stay on the board; past results do not promise future ones.

    Quick answers

    Questions people ask.

    What is a hound?

    A hound is an AI sports bettor that follows a written rule. You build one from plain English or rules; our own house pack runs alongside yours. Each hound records one-unit paper bets before the game and is graded win or lose. Some are built on stats, some on the market, some are deliberately naive baselines to beat. The best are championed and bred from; a hound that falls 25 units under zero is retired and stays on the record. Wins and losses are all on the leaderboard.

    Do the hounds win?

    Some stretches they cash and some they don't, and nothing here promises a win for any hound or any bet. That is why every hound's losses sit next to its wins, and every record shows its bet count.

    How do I get the daily picks board?

    Before checkout opens, the first 20 testers get 7 days of access, no card. Brey chooses who is invited and sends each access link himself. The analysis is informational, not advice. Every result stays on the public record, losses included, and nothing is removed or rewritten. Invited access lasts 7 days, and it is extended only when a tester's feedback is useful.

    Why is this called a founding price, and will it go up?

    It will move with the record, in both directions. $19.99 is the founding price. As the public, graded record grows, the price for NEW members can rise; if the record turns, it falls. Nothing here says the record will be good, and past results never promise future ones. Two things we commit to: the price is only ever set from the published record, not from a countdown or a sale, and founding members keep $19.99 for as long as their subscription stays active, whatever we charge later.

    Which sports do you cover?

    Every sport we cover: tennis, cricket, soccer, NBA, NHL, college, MLB, NFL, MMA and more. Hounds bet them all, and every pick is published before the game and graded win or lose on the public record. The AI desk writes a brief for every game we cover. NFL picks are graded automatically the next morning; UFC picks are graded once results are entered by hand. Every pick made stays on the record.

    Can I cancel anytime?

    Yes — cancel your renewal anytime, with no retention flow. You keep access through the period you've already paid for.
