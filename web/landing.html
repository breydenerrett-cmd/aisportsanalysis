How it works

Three steps. Nothing hidden.

1. Posted
Every pick, before the game.

The pick, how likely it is — by the whole market and by our own numbers — and what the price needs to break even, written to a public, hash-chained record the moment it's made.

2. Graded
Win, lose, or void — said plainly.

Graded exactly as it stood at its lock. Nothing is re-graded, re-ranked, or quietly removed after the fact.

3. Kept on the record
Losses stay, same size as the wins.

Every graded night sits on one public page you can open yourself — not a highlight reel of the nights we were right.

Why we publish the losses

Between 2023 and 2024 we pre-registered a long list of separate research ideas against MLB moneyline betting — 37 tested so far, from bullpen workload and platoon matchups to market-structure effects like fading recent price moves. Zero have survived our own falsification checks. We publish that too, at the same size as everything else.

Fair questions

Frequently asked questions.

No guaranteed wins — is that really true?

Yes. Nothing here guarantees an outcome or a profit, for any user or any bet. You are responsible for your own wagering decisions, including whether to bet at all.

Is this gambling advice?

No. We publish our own picks with how likely they are and what the price needs, and we publish how they do. Whether to bet, how much, and where is your decision, and nothing here is a guarantee.

Do you predict who wins?

We say which side is more likely — by the whole market and by our own numbers — before first pitch, and we grade it after. That is not a promised winner: a 64% side loses more than a third of the time, and the record shows it.

If you already know who's more likely, why does the price matter?

Because a likely side at a bad price still loses money. Every pick shows the win rate the price needs to break even next to the likelihood we actually have; if the price needs more than the side is likely to deliver, the pick says so.

No betting edge found yet — what does that mean?

Exactly what it says. We've pre-registered and tested 37 distinct research hypotheses against real MLB games from 2023–2024, and none survived our falsification tests. We publish the losers, including the one that looked promising until we checked it properly.

Which sports do you cover?

MLB, NFL and UFC carry live picks, published and graded the same way on the same record. Tennis is research-stage — a match board, not picks yet.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes — cancel your renewal anytime, with no retention flow. You keep access through the period you've already paid for.

Why don't you show a win percentage like other apps do?

Because we do not have one we can stand behind. Our own research found zero surviving betting ideas against real games. We will not publish a win probability that has never been validated.
