Why we built this

We bet too. Most ideas don't survive contact with real games.

Between 2023 and 2024 we pre-registered four separate research families against MLB moneyline betting — 25 distinct ideas at the detector level (35 counting every registered variant), from bullpen workload and platoon matchups to market-structure effects like fading recent price moves and cross-book price dispersion.

Zero of them survived our own falsification tests. One idea looked like a real signal at first — a positive, statistically significant effect over 249 selections — until the pre-committed checks showed it came almost entirely from one sportsbook and reversed direction just below the threshold that defined it. We killed it and kept the record.

  • We don't ship a pick because one candidate idea looked good in a backtest — every idea here failed a pre-committed replication test before it could reach the product.
  • Nothing here states a win probability or a predicted winner. That's not a feature we haven't built yet — it's a rule, and no field in the product emits one.
  • What we do give you — the quoted prices, the market-implied consensus, and what changed recently — is real, checkable, and never dressed up as more than it is.

What line shopping is actually worth.

The same bet is often quoted at different prices by different books. Taking the better one costs you nothing if the bet loses, and pays you more if it wins — no prediction, no probability, just the arithmetic of two prices on the same bet.

  • If the bet wins: the better price pays more.
  • If the bet loses: both prices lose the same stake — the difference is $0.

A better price also lowers the win rate you need to break even — at −110 you need 52.4% of these bets to win to break even; at −105 you need only 51.2%. Pure price arithmetic; no assumed win rate goes into it.

We never name which book had the best price on this page — on 63–79% of observed instants, more than one book was tied for it, so naming one would be arbitrary. A quoted price is what we observed, not a guarantee that book will take your exact bet at your exact size.

Fair questions

Frequently asked questions.

No guaranteed wins — is that really true?

Yes. Nothing here guarantees an outcome or a profit, for any user or any bet. You are responsible for your own wagering decisions, including whether to bet at all.

Is this gambling advice?

No. We show prices, market-implied consensus, and what changed — not a recommendation. The product's own recommendation field is intentionally and permanently empty until a validated prediction engine clears our own bar.

Do you predict who wins?

No. We do not publish a win probability or a predicted winner anywhere in the product. That is a permanent rule, not a gap we are planning to fill.

What's a "price improvement," then?

The better of two quoted prices for the same bet — line shopping, not a prediction. See "Find the better number" above.

No betting edge found yet — what does that mean?

Exactly what it says. We've pre-registered and tested 25 distinct research ideas (35 counting every variant) against real MLB games from 2023–2024, and none survived our falsification tests. We publish the losers, including the one that looked promising until we checked it properly.

How fresh is the data?

Every price and consensus figure carries the timestamp of when we last observed it, and we show its age rather than hiding it. If there's no market currently priced for a game, we say that plainly instead of showing a stale or fabricated number.

Which sports do you cover?

MLB only, today. That's where our research and data pipeline are built out; other sports are not currently supported.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes — cancel your renewal anytime, with no retention flow. You keep access through the period you've already paid for.

How many free Bet Checks do I get?

Three, total, for the lifetime of your account — not three per day. No card required to use them.

Why don't you show a win percentage like other apps do?

Because we do not have one we can stand behind. Our own research found zero surviving betting ideas against real games. We will not publish a win probability that has never been validated.
