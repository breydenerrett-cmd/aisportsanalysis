How it works, and what is proven

What is proven and what is not.

  • Proven
    Every pick is published before the game: the pick, how likely it is by the whole market and by our own numbers, and what the price needs to break even. The ledger is hash-chained, so a pick cannot be added or changed afterwards without breaking the chain.
  • Proven
    Every pick is graded exactly as it was published: a win, a loss or a void, said plainly. Nothing is re-graded, re-ranked, removed or edited after the fact.
  • Result so far
    The record so far has not shown that the picks make money. The current figures are at the top of this page.
  • Experimental
    None of the research ideas we have tested has survived our own checks.
  • No track record
    The postseason odds are model estimates with no track record yet.

Losses stay on the record at the same size as the wins. Every graded night sits on one public page you can open yourself, not a highlight reel of the nights we were right.

We pre-registered a long list of separate research ideas, from bullpen workload and platoon matchups to market-structure effects like fading recent price moves, and tested them against past MLB moneyline games. We publish that too, at the same size as everything else.

Fair questions

Frequently asked questions.

What is early access?

Before checkout opens, the first 20 testers get 7 days of access, no card. Brey chooses who is invited and sends each access link himself. It is early access: performance is not proven, and the analysis is informational, not advice. Every result stays on the public record, losses included, and nothing is removed or rewritten. Tester access is temporary: it lasts 7 days, and it is extended only when a tester's feedback is useful.

Why is this called a founding price, and will it go up?

It will move with the record, in both directions. $19.99 is what early members pay while the record is young. As the public, graded record grows, the price for NEW members can rise; if the record turns, it falls. Nothing here says the record will be good, and past results never promise future ones. Two things we commit to: the price is only ever set from the published record, not from a countdown or a sale, and founding members keep $19.99 for as long as their subscription stays active, whatever we charge later.

No guaranteed wins — is that really true?

Yes. Nothing here guarantees an outcome or a profit, for any user or any bet. You are responsible for your own wagering decisions, including whether to bet at all.

Is this gambling advice?

No. We publish our own picks with how likely they are and what the price needs, and we publish how they do. Whether to bet, how much, and where is your decision, and nothing here is a guarantee.

Do you predict who wins?

We say which side is more likely — by the whole market and by our own numbers — before first pitch, and we grade it after. That is not a promised winner: a 64% side loses more than a third of the time, and the record shows it.

If you already know who's more likely, why does the price matter?

Because a likely side at a bad price still loses money. Every pick shows the win rate the price needs to break even next to the likelihood we actually have; if the price needs more than the side is likely to deliver, the pick says so.

No betting edge found yet — what does that mean?

Exactly what it says. In 2026 we pre-registered and tested 37 distinct research hypotheses against real MLB games from 2023–2024, and none survived our falsification tests. We publish the losers, including the one that looked promising until we checked it properly.

Which sports do you cover?

MLB, NFL and UFC all carry live picks, published before the game or event. MLB and NFL grade automatically the next morning; UFC picks grade once results are entered by hand. Tennis is research-stage — a match board, not picks yet.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes — cancel your renewal anytime, with no retention flow. You keep access through the period you've already paid for.

Why don't you show a win percentage like other apps do?

We do — our own number sits right next to the market's on every pick. It is unvalidated: our own research found zero surviving betting ideas against real games, and every pick is graded in public regardless.
