    How it works, in three steps.

    1. 1

      Pick or build a model.

      Start from a house model or say a strategy in plain English. It becomes a model with a written rule.

    2. 2

      It makes paper bets.

      Each ticket is one unit of paper money, written down before the game. No real money is ever staked.

    3. 3

      We grade it in public.

      The next morning every ticket is marked won or lost. Wins and losses stay on the board; past results do not promise future ones.

    Quick answers

    Questions people ask.

    What are the models?

    Each model follows a written rule, records one-unit paper bets before the game, and is graded the next morning. Some are built on stats, some on the market, some are deliberately naive baselines to beat. A model that falls 25 units under zero is retired and stays on the record. Wins and losses are all on the leaderboard.

    Do the models win?

    Some stretches they cash and some they don't, and nothing here promises a win for any model or any bet. That is why every model's losses sit next to its wins, and why a record on a small number of bets is labelled as such.

    What is early access?

    Before checkout opens, the first 20 testers get 7 days of access, no card. Brey chooses who is invited and sends each access link himself. It is early access: performance is not proven, and the analysis is informational, not advice. Every result stays on the public record, losses included, and nothing is removed or rewritten. Tester access is temporary: it lasts 7 days, and it is extended only when a tester's feedback is useful.

    Why is this called a founding price, and will it go up?

    It will move with the record, in both directions. $19.99 is what early members pay while the record is young. As the public, graded record grows, the price for NEW members can rise; if the record turns, it falls. Nothing here says the record will be good, and past results never promise future ones. Two things we commit to: the price is only ever set from the published record, not from a countdown or a sale, and founding members keep $19.99 for as long as their subscription stays active, whatever we charge later.

    Which sports do you cover?

    The models run on MLB and NFL. The AI desk writes a brief for every MLB game we cover; MLB value picks are paused as of Oct 16, 2026, and the picks already made stay on the record. NFL carries live picks, published before the game and graded automatically the next morning. UFC picks are paused while we build real fight analysis; the UFC picks already made are graded once results are entered by hand and stay on the record. Tennis is research only: a match board, not picks yet.

    Can I cancel anytime?

    Yes — cancel your renewal anytime, with no retention flow. You keep access through the period you've already paid for.
